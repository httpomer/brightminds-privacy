Privacy Policy

Bright Minds — learning & play for little ones

Effective date: 21 July 2026  ·  Last updated: 29 September 2026

Bright Minds ("the app") is a learning-and-play app for young children, published by WeaverSol LLC ("we", "us"). Package name: com.nthsol.brightminds.

This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with information. We have written it to be read by parents.

The short version: Bright Minds collects no personal information from children. The free app shows a small number of child-safe ads from Google AdMob, which are never personalised and never use the advertising ID (see Section 4); a parent can remove them with an optional monthly subscription. A parent may optionally create an account — email address and password — to back up their children's progress; that is asked for only behind the Parent Zone's grown-up check, it is entirely skippable, and every part of the app works without one. Your children's names and their drawings never leave the device. Two optional drawing features send data to outside services in order to work. All of this is described in full below.

1. Information we do not collect

The app does not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • Phone numbers or postal addresses
  • Dates of birth
  • Photographs, camera images, or video
  • Voice or microphone recordings
  • Location or GPS data
  • Contacts, calendar entries, or files from the device
  • The Android advertising ID — the app does not even request permission to read it — or any persistent tracking identifier of our own

The app contains exactly two places where anything can be typed, and neither is used to collect information about a child for us:

  1. A parent's email address and password, on the optional account screen. That screen sits behind the Parent Zone's grown-up check and is never shown to a child. See Section 2A.
  2. A child's first name, asked once when setting a profile up. It is optional — skipping it simply names the profile after the chosen buddy — and it is stored only on this device and never transmitted, not even when a parent has an account.

We use no analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking software of any kind. The only advertising software in the app is Google AdMob, set up for children as described in Section 4; it is not used to build a profile of your child.

2A. The optional parent account

A parent may create an account so that their children's progress survives a lost phone, a new device, or a reinstall. It is entirely optional. Every game, story, drawing tool and setting works exactly the same without one, and the app never blocks a child from playing in order to ask for it.

The account screen is reached only through the Parent Zone, which is protected by a grown-up check. We never ask a child to create an account, and never ask a child for an email address.

What we collect if — and only if — a parent creates an account:

Email address To identify the account and let the parent sign back in

Marketing preference, plus the date it was given To honour the choice, and to evidence it

What is backed up to our servers when signed in:

  • Each child's buddy character and age band
  • Each child's progress: stars, levels, streak, play-time counters, outfit
  • The household settings (voice, easy mode, reduced motion, daily limit)

What is never backed up, whether or not you have an account:

  • Your children's names. A typed first name stays on the device.
  • Your children's drawings. They stay on the device.

Marketing email. The tick box on the signup screen is switched off by default and is a separate choice from creating the account — you can create one without it. If you tick it, we may email you about other apps we make. Every email carries an unsubscribe link, and you can also switch it off any time in Parent Zone → Account. We do not sell or share your address with anyone.

Deleting the account. Parent Zone → Account → Delete my account removes the account and the backup from our servers. Your children, their progress and their drawings remain on your device. You can also email us at m.irfanshkh@gmail.com.

Signing out removes nothing: every child, their progress and their drawings stay on the device.

3. Features that connect to the internet

Two features need an internet connection. Everything else in the app works offline.

3.1 Auto Draw (doodle recognition)

In Drawing → Auto Draw, your child doodles and the app tries to guess what they drew.

To do this, the app sends the shape of the drawing — the coordinates of the lines drawn and the size of the drawing area — to Google's handwriting recognition service at www.google.com/inputtools. Google returns a list of guessed words.

  • Sent: line coordinates and canvas dimensions only.
  • Not sent: any name, identifier, device information, or account detail. The request carries nothing that identifies your child or your device.
  • The drawing is sent as a set of coordinates, not as a picture or a file.

This data is handled by Google under Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

If you would rather this feature was not used, simply do not open Auto Draw.

3.2 Colouring Book (picture library)

In Drawing → Colouring Book, the app fetches line-art pictures to colour in from Pixabay, a stock image library, via its public API.

  • Sent: a search word chosen by the app itself (for example "animals" or "flowers") and our developer API key.
  • Not sent: anything typed by your child (nothing can be typed), and no identifier for your child or device.
  • Pixabay's SafeSearch filter is switched on for every request the app makes.
  • Downloaded pictures are cached on the device so they load quickly next time.

Pixabay handles these requests under its own privacy policy: https://pixabay.com/service/privacy/

Pictures come from a third-party library. While SafeSearch is always enabled and the app requests only illustration-style images, we do not control Pixabay's catalogue. We recommend the Colouring Book be used with normal parental awareness, as with any app that shows images from the internet.

3.3 Spoken voice guidance

The app reads questions and encouragement aloud using the text-to-speech voice built into your device's operating system (Android or iOS). The words spoken are fixed phrases written by us — for example "How many do you see?".

Nothing your child says or does is recorded: the app has no microphone permission and cannot listen. Depending on your device's own settings, your operating system's speech engine may process the text to be spoken; that is governed by your device manufacturer's privacy policy, not by us.

Voice guidance can be switched off at any time in the Parent Zone.

3.4 Account backup (only if a parent creates an account)

If — and only if — a parent creates an account, the app connects to Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to hold the account and the backup described in Section 2A. Data is stored in the United States (Google's nam5 multi-region). Google processes it as our service provider under its own terms: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy and https://policies.google.com/privacy

With no account, the app makes no connection to Firebase at all.

4. Advertising and purchases

4.1 How ads appear

Bright Minds is free and is supported by ads from Google AdMob, a Google Play Families self-certified ads SDK. Ads appear in three places only:

  • a banner on menu screens (the home screen, game pickers, level lists and collections) — never on a game, story, or drawing screen while a child is playing;
  • occasionally a full-screen ad at a natural break, after a level or story is finished — never when the app opens, never in the middle of an activity, at most once every three minutes and no more than once every two completed levels;
  • an optional video that a child can choose to watch for a small bonus, or to skip a level they are stuck on. It only ever plays when the button is tapped.

4.2 How the ads are set up for children

Before any ad is requested, the app tells Google that every request is:

  • directed to children (COPPA "child-directed treatment"),
  • from a user under the age of consent (for the EU/UK and similar laws),
  • limited to ads rated "G" (general audiences) only.

With these settings Google does not show personalised (interest-based) ads, does not use remarketing, and applies its policies for child-directed content. The app also asks for non-personalised ads on every request, and it does not request the Android advertising ID or the Android "Privacy Sandbox" ad APIs.

4.3 What Google processes to show an ad

To deliver an ad, count how many times it has been shown, and prevent fraud, Google's SDK processes technical information such as the device's IP address (which can indicate an approximate region), the device model and operating system, the app version, and whether an ad was shown, tapped or watched. This is handled by Google under its own policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6223431 (child-directed treatment). We do not receive information that identifies your child.

4.4 Purchases

  • Bright Minds offers one optional in-app purchase: "Premium", a monthly subscription that removes every ad from the app. It renews and is charged every month until it is cancelled, which a parent can do at any time in their Google Play (or App Store) account under Subscriptions. Every game and activity works the same without it.
  • The subscription is offered only inside the Parent Zone, behind the grown-up check. A child is never shown a price or a buy button.
  • Payment is handled entirely by Google Play (or the App Store). We never see or store your card or payment details.
  • To know whether a subscription is currently active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription-management service. RevenueCat receives the purchase record from the store (what was bought and when, and whether it is active) against a random, anonymous identifier created by the app. It does not receive a name, an email address, or anything about your child. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
  • With Premium, the app stops requesting ads altogether, so the processing described in Section 4.3 no longer takes place.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any information with data brokers, advertisers, or marketing companies.
  • If a parent has ticked the marketing box when creating an account, we may send that parent occasional email about other apps we make. This is never sent to a child, the tick box is off by default, every email has an unsubscribe link, and it can be switched off in Parent Zone → Account.

5. Permissions the app requests

On Android, the only permission that matters to your privacy is:

  • Internet access — used for the features in Section 3 and to show the ads in Section 4.

Google's libraries also add a few routine system permissions (checking whether the device is online, keeping the device awake while a download finishes, and similar) that do not give access to any personal information. The app does not request the advertising ID, and does not request access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photo library, or device storage. On iOS, the app declares no permission requests at all.

6. Children's privacy

Bright Minds is designed for children aged approximately 2.5 and older, and is intended to be installed and set up by a parent or guardian.

We have deliberately built the app so that no personal information is collected from children. This is intended to meet the requirements of the United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) including its provisions on children's data, and Google Play's Families policy.

Specifically:

  • An email address and password are requested only from a parent, only behind the Parent Zone's grown-up check, and are never requested from a child or about a child. The account belongs to the adult, not to the child.
  • A child's first name is optional and never transmitted. It is stored in the app's private storage on the device and is deliberately excluded from the account backup, so we never hold it.
  • A child's drawings are never transmitted.
  • No account is ever required to use the app, so a child is never prevented from playing because a parent chose not to provide personal information.
  • Ads are child-directed, G-rated and never personalised (Section 4), and the app does not request the advertising ID.

The only personal data we ever hold on a server is the parent's own email address, given deliberately by that parent.

The Parent Zone — containing play-time limits, usage information, child management, account settings, and the child switcher when a daily limit is in force — is protected behind a multiplication question chosen to be beyond the arithmetic the app itself teaches, so that young children cannot get past it.

If you believe your child has somehow provided us with personal information, please contact us at m.irfanshkh@gmail.com and we will look into it promptly.

7. Data retention and security

On the device. Your children's names, drawings and progress stay in the app's private storage on your own device, protected by your device's own protections (such as your screen lock and the operating system's app sandboxing). This data is kept until you clear the app's data, delete a child in the Parent Zone, or uninstall the app.

On our servers. If a parent has created an account, we hold that parent's email address, their marketing preference, and the backup described in Section 2A with Google Firebase in the United States region (Google's nam5 multi-region). Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us. Access is restricted so that an account can only ever read or write its own household's data. Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS and is encrypted at rest by Google.

We keep account data until the account is deleted, either in Parent Zone → Account → Delete my account or by request to m.irfanshkh@gmail.com. Marketing preferences are kept until withdrawn, and for a reasonable period afterwards to evidence that the request was honoured.

Requests to the services in Section 3 are all sent over encrypted HTTPS connections.

If you have not created an account, we hold no personal data about you or your child at all, so there is nothing for us to provide or erase. You control everything on your device and can remove it at any time (see Section 2).

If you have created an account, you can exercise those rights directly in the app:
